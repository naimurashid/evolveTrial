  values the R dispatcher passes, after the same coercions (`E_C`, `E_T` reach it as `arma::ivec`). Do not rewrite

  `norm_rand` with a hand-applied affine transform unless you show it is bitwise what `rnorm` does.

  but must all be made, in order.
- The medians (`calculate_median_survival_piecewise` on `lambda` and `lambda * exp_beta`) draw nothing. You may
  skip computing them only after showing that nothing downstream reads `medCtrl` or `medTrt` at the interim, at
